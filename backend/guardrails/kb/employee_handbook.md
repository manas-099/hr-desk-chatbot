## 1. Administrative Governance & Employment Classifications

## 1.1 Executive Intent & Scope

Quilltony Technologies is committed to maintaining transparent, equitable, and legally compliant operational standards across   all   operating   entities.   This   Handbook   codifies   core   employment   parameters,   operational   paradigms,   and organizational expectations for all personnel. Unless explicitly modified by a localized entity addendum in Section 12, these policies apply universally across all global operating divisions.

## 1.2 FLSA Exempt vs. Non-Exempt Classifications

In strict compliance with the United States Fair Labor Standards Act (FLSA) and corresponding international labor codes, Quilltony Technologies categorizes every position based on duties, autonomy, and compensation thresholds:

- Exempt Personnel: Positions primarily professional, executive, or administrative in scope. Exempt employees are paid a fixed salary and are not eligible for statutory overtime premium pay. ·
- Non-Exempt Personnel: Positions subject to statutory minimum wage and overtime mandates. Non-exempt employees must record all working hours accurately and receive 1.5x base hourly pay for hours worked exceeding 40 in a single workweek. ·

| Classification Tier           | FLSA Status   | Standard Workweek          | Overtime Authorization Requirement         |
|-------------------------------|---------------|----------------------------|--------------------------------------------|
| Full-Time Regular (FTE 1.0)   | Exempt        | 40 Hours Nominal           | N/A (Salaried Output Model)                |
| Full-Time Hourly (FTE 1.0)    | Non-Exempt    | 40 Hours Strictly Recorded | Prior Written Approval from Direct Manager |
| Part-Time Regular (FTE < 0.8) | Non-Exempt    | < 30 Hours Recorded        | Mandatory Executive Approval Over 30 Hours |

## 2. Code of Business Conduct & Whistleblower Protections

Quilltony Technologies enforces a zero-tolerance mandate regarding corrupt, illegal, or unethical business practices. Personnel are strictly required to avoid conflicts of interest, safeguard company assets, and comply with all anti-bribery statutes (including the US Foreign Corrupt Practices Act and UK Bribery Act). Reports of misconduct can be submitted anonymously through the Quilltony Governance Portal at ethics.quilltony.com . Retaliation against any employee reporting a violation in good faith is strictly prohibited and subject to immediate termination.

## 3. Equal Opportunity, Anti-Harassment, & Workplace Civility

Quilltony Technologies provides equal employment opportunities to all individuals regardless of race, color, religion, sex, national origin, age, disability, genetics, sexual orientation, or gender identity. Sexual harassment, verbal abuse, and bullying are rigorously enforced against. Escalation follows a three-step protocol:

- Direct informal resolution if safe and appropriate; 1.
- Formal escalation to People Operations via peopleops@quilltony.com ; 2.
- External independent audit if executive leadership is implicated. 3.

## 4. Asynchronous Communication & Remote Operations

As a remote-first organization, Quilltony Technologies prioritizes asynchronous collaboration over real-time meetings. Core collaboration windows are restricted to 10:00 AM to 2:00 PM EST. All operational decisions, architectural choices, and strategy updates must be recorded in internal wiki repositories rather than isolated private messaging channels.

Paid Time Off (PTO) at Quilltony Technologies accrues per semi-monthly pay period based on completed length of continuous service. Accrual transitions take effect on the first day of the pay period following the milestone anniversary.

## 6. Parental Leave & Family Caregiving Top-Up Coordination

Quilltony Technologies provides up to 16 continuous weeks of fully paid Parental Leave to eligible regular employees following the birth, adoption, or foster placement of a child. Company top-up pay coordinates with state statutory benefits (such as California PFL or New York PFL) to ensure the employee receives 100% of their base salary during the approved 16-week window.

## 7. Group Health, Welfare, & Retirement Benefits

All regular full-time employees working 30+ hours per week are eligible for medical, dental, and vision coverage effective the first day of the calendar month following employment commencement. Quilltony Technologies sponsors a 401(k) retirement plan featuring a 100% dollar-for-dollar employer match up to 4% of eligible annual earnings, fully vested immediately upon contribution.

## 8. Professional Development & Growth Stipends

Employees are allocated an annual Professional Growth Stipend up to $3,500 per fiscal year. Expense approvals require prior manager authorization and must directly relate to the employee's current professional domain or defined career ladder trajectory at Quilltony Technologies.

## 9. Travel, Hospitality, & Business Expense Governance

Reimbursable business travel requires advance approval. Per diem lodging and meal caps are fixed by location tier (Tier 1 Metro: $350/night lodging, $85/day meals; Tier 2/3: $220/night lodging, $65/day meals). Alcohol is non-reimbursable except during formal corporate events approved by a Vice President.

## 10. IT Security, Asset Management, & Acceptable Use

All corporate laptops must utilize FileVault/BitLocker full-disk encryption and maintain continuous connections to Quilltony MDM solutions. Passwords must adhere to a 16-character minimum requirement. Personal storage devices are strictly blocked on enterprise machines.

## 12. Jurisdictional Addenda & Statutory Overrides

Where local, state, or national laws conflict with general baseline policies established in Sections 1 through 10 of this Handbook, statutory mandates take precedence. The following entity addenda explicitly override baseline parameters:

## 12.1 California Statutory Addendum

- PTO Non-Forfeiture Override: Notwithstanding Section 5.1, accrued PTO for California-based personnel never expires and cannot be forfeited under any "use-it-or-lose-it" scheme. Accrual pauses only if total accrued balance hits the maximum cap (1.5x annual rate). Unused PTO must be paid out in full upon separation at final rate of pay. ·
- Mandatory Rest Breaks: Non-exempt California employees receive a net 10-minute paid rest break for every 4 hours worked. ·

## 12.2 United Kingdom Entity Addendum

- Statutory Leave Alignment: UK personnel receive 28 days of paid annual leave inclusive of public bank holidays, superseding standard baseline accrual tables in Section 5. ·
