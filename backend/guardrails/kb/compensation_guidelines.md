## 1. Global Compensation Philosophy & Market Benchmarking

Quilltony Technologies maintains a strict formulaic approach to total rewards. Our compensation structure targets the 75th percentile of the market for equivalent technology roles, benchmarked annually against the Radford/Aon Global Technology Survey. By removing discretionary negotiation, Quilltony Technologies ensures absolute internal equity across gender, racial, and geographic demographics.

## 2. The Total Base Compensation Formula Architecture

Base cash compensation for all non-executive personnel is calculated deterministically using the following formula:

Total Base Salary = Role Benchmark Base × Level Factor × Geographic Location Multiplier

No hiring manager, department head, or recruiter possesses authorization to override outputs generated by this formula.

## 3. Career Architecture & Job Leveling Framework

Our dual-track career progression architecture differentiates Individual Contributor (IC) technical mastery from People Management (M) scope. Roles progress from IC-2 (Associate) through IC-6 (Principal), and M-4 (Manager) through M-5 (Senior Director).

## 6. Equity Incentive Plans & Vesting Governance

Equity grants consist of Restricted Stock Units (RSUs) or Stock Options depending on corporate tax structure in the employee's jurisdiction. Initial onboarding equity grants vest over 4 years under a standard schedule: 25% vesting cliff at Month 12 , followed by linear 1/48th monthly vesting thereafter. Annual refresher grants carry no cliff and vest quarterly over 4 years (1/16th per quarter).

## 7. Administrative Governance, Reviews, & Zero-Negotiation Rules

## 7.1 Zero-Negotiation Policy

Quilltony Technologies maintains a strict zero-negotiation policy on all initial job offers. All candidate offers are generated strictly   via   the   mathematical   formula  defined  in  Section  2.  Counter-offers or discretionary salary bumps are strictly rejected to eliminate systemic wage gaps.

## 7.2 Promotion Jump Floor

Any employee receiving a formal career level promotion (e.g., IC-3 to IC-4) is guaranteed a total base compensation increase equal to at least 8.0% or movement to the minimum band threshold of the new job level, whichever is greater.
